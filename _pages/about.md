I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at [Carlson School of Management](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/), University of Minnesota. My research focuses on **algorithmic decision-making**. Specifically, I design machine/deep learning algorithms to support human judgment in a data-intensive environment. I am also interested in examining the impact of public policies and human behaviors on the use of AI systems in society.

I have been actively engaged in research topics such as **recommender systems**, **data privacy regulations**, **AI in healthcare**, and **generative AI**.

Before joining the Carlson School of Management, I obtained my M.Sc. in Data Science from [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/) and my bachelor's degree in Information Systems from the [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/).
